# Listing Rescue Sprint

## Who it is for

UK corporate eBay sellers with dozens or hundreds of active listings and visible inconsistencies that can affect search matching or buyer confidence.

## £99 pilot — delivered within 24 hours

- 15 search-aware title rewrites using only verified product facts
- item-specific QA flags for missing, contradictory or unclear specifics
- ready-to-paste structured descriptions
- one CSV or spreadsheet handoff
- one revision pass
- no eBay password or account access required

## £179 sprint

The same deliverable for 40 listings, offered only after the buyer sees the pilot format.

## Buyer inputs

- 15 public listing URLs or a seller-store URL
- any private product facts that should be included
- preferred tone and required disclaimers

## Boundaries

- No fabricated specifications, provenance, condition, brand claims or search terms.
- No claim that copy changes guarantee ranking or sales.
- No marketplace credential handling.
- The client reviews every change before publishing.

## Sales process

1. Provide one concrete public-listing observation in the first message.
2. Offer a three-listing preview before commitment.
3. On interest, confirm scope and the user's legitimate payment route.
4. Deliver the three-listing preview, then collect the agreed payment/deposit before the remaining batch unless the buyer requires another reasonable structure.
