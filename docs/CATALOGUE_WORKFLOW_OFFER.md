# £399 Supplier-Spec-to-Catalogue Workflow

Prepared: 2026-09-10

## Buyer

Established e-commerce brands with roughly 100 or more products, inconsistent legacy descriptions, and usable supplier specifications or product data that have not been turned into consistent customer-facing copy.

## Purchase trigger

- A catalogue migration, new supplier range, seasonal upload, marketplace expansion, or visible backlog.
- Multiple writers or imports have produced inconsistent structure and tone.
- The team wants a repeatable reviewed workflow, not a full storefront rebuild or unreviewed bulk AI output.

## £399 pilot

- Source-field audit for one supplier spreadsheet/CSV and supporting specification files.
- Approved fact map separating usable facts, missing fields, prohibited claims, and review questions.
- Brand-voice and description-structure guide.
- Deterministic preprocessing and QA workflow.
- Twenty-five reviewed product titles and descriptions in an import-ready CSV.
- Exception report for missing, contradictory, or unsafe product data.
- One revision pass.
- Delivery within 48 hours after cleared payment and receipt of usable inputs.

## £799 expansion

Up to 100 products using the approved schema and tone after the 25-product pilot is accepted. Offer only after the input quality, category, and review burden are known.

## Value-first proof

Before commitment, process three representative products supplied by the prospect or selected from public product pages. Show the source facts, draft output, and QA flags side by side. Do not publish anything and do not request store credentials.

## Boundaries

- Every factual, technical, safety, ingredient, compatibility, provenance, warranty, environmental, medical, legal, or performance claim must come from buyer-approved source data.
- No invented reviews, testimonials, scarcity, certifications, rankings, guarantees, or comparison claims.
- No automatic publishing. The buyer reviews every row before import.
- Do not process personal data, credentials, private customer data, or copyrighted supplier material the buyer is not authorised to use.
- Regulated or safety-sensitive categories require a narrower scope or are declined.
- The included script performs deterministic structure and QA checks; it is not a substitute for factual or legal review.

## Activation

- Offer as an expansion when a primary prospect has a large catalogue and explicitly mentions inconsistent supplier data, repeated manual copy, or a backlog.
- Consider a direct five-prospect test only after the primary and secondary observation gates, with public evidence and a corporate channel.
- Do not apply through an ID-dependent marketplace for this mission.

This is evidence that the problem and higher-value scope exist; it is not a client, lead, won project, or pricing guarantee.
