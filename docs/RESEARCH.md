# Day-1 Opportunity Research

Research date: 2026-09-10. Current facts are sourced; commercial probabilities and scores are explicit estimates.

## Scoring model

Each opportunity is rated 1–5 on: seven-day cash chance (25%), margin (15%), speed (15%), AI advantage (15%), accessible distribution (10%), observed demand evidence (10%), repeatability (5%), and capital safety (5%). The resulting score is a prioritisation aid, not a probability or forecast.

## Evidence that controls the selection

- Direct, honest B2B outreach to a corporate subscriber can be conducted without PECR consent, while UK GDPR still applies when personal data is used. Corporate-only targeting, relevance, identification, opt-out handling, and a suppression log are required.
- Upwork visibly carries recent demand for product descriptions, spreadsheet/data cleaning, automation and technical SEO, but fixed-price funds clear only after client approval plus a five-day security period. A new marketplace profile also has a trust and competition barrier.
- Fiverr releases 80% of cleared client payment and normally holds seller funds for 14 days after completion, outside the mission window.
- PeoplePerHour can apply an automated clearing period of up to 14 working days.
- Gumroad direct sales carry platform and payment-processing fees, and standard payouts impose at least a seven-day holding period; new UK creator setup is therefore a poor seven-day cash path.
- UserTesting normally pays 14 days after completion. Respondent typically takes 7–10 business days after attendance confirmation and pays through Tremendous. Both are too slow for the primary scoreboard.
- Prolific can pay approved balances to PayPal within 24 hours after cash-out, but researcher approval can take up to 22 days and participant eligibility is unknown.
- UK private eBay selling currently has no transaction fee in most categories. Funds generally become available after delivery, or sooner for eligible established sellers, then may take 0–4 business days to reach a bank. This is viable only if the user owns saleable inventory and can ship immediately.
- Public eBay evidence shows active UK corporate sellers with large catalogues and concrete listing-quality gaps: inconsistent title syntax, missing searchable specifics, unexplained condition codes, stale storefront copy, or seller-description sections that expose little usable copy.
- A live Upwork brief checked on 2026-09-10 offers $900 fixed price for a workflow that converts supplier specifications into consistent, reviewed Shopify descriptions across about 180 products. A current Freelancer category page also shows dozens of open product-description and listing projects, including bulk catalogue work. This supports demand for a higher-value catalogue workflow, while the marketplace identity, competition, and payout constraints still make direct selling preferable for this mission.
- The user's existing public Lane repository is relevant proof of UK eBay/Vinted workflow capability, but it still requires marketplace credentials and a working deployment for live onboarding.

## Selected portfolio

### Primary — E-commerce listing rescue sprint

Buyer: UK limited companies with active marketplace catalogues and publicly observable listing defects.

Offer: 15 listings in 24 hours for £99: title rewrites, item-specific QA flags, and ready-to-paste descriptions. No marketplace login required. A £179 tier covers 40 listings after the first pilot.

Reason: one sale clears the £70 floor at £0 additional spend; fulfilment is fast; value can be demonstrated before purchase; the existing Lane work makes the niche credible.

Initial funnel assumption: 3% close rate across highly qualified corporate prospects. At that assumption, 34 contacts are required for one sale. This is an estimate to test, not observed performance. Pivot after 10 qualified attempts with no engagement; reassess the offer/target after 20 with no meaningful response.

### Secondary — Landing-page conversion patch

Buyer: small service or SaaS companies with visible unclear positioning or weak calls to action.

Offer: annotated audit plus rewritten hero, CTA, FAQ and implementation patch for £149 within 24 hours.

Activation trigger: primary batch reaches 10 qualified contacts without engagement, or an e-commerce prospect has a clearer website-conversion problem than a listing problem.

### Asymmetric experiment — supplier-spec-to-catalogue workflow

Offer: £399 for a source-field audit, approved fact map, deterministic QA workflow, brand structure, and 25 reviewed product drafts; expand to £799 for up to 100 products only after the pilot is accepted.

Activation trigger: a primary prospect or other established brand explicitly mentions inconsistent supplier data, a copy backlog, multiple legacy writers, or a large catalogue migration. The Lane onboarding offer moves to reserve because its dependency and credential burden is higher.

### Emergency fallback — sell existing user-owned inventory

Target: one or more unused owned items with an aggregate realistic sale value of at least £90, listed accurately on eBay UK with immediate dispatch. This needs user-supplied inventory/photos and cannot be invented or purchased speculatively.

## Revenue arithmetic

- £99 primary pilot: one sale yields £99 gross; assumed direct bank transfer fee £0; net £99 before tax. Payment method is not yet verified.
- £179 primary sprint: one sale yields £179 gross; assumed direct bank transfer fee £0.
- £149 secondary patch: one sale yields £149 gross; assumed direct bank transfer fee £0.
- £399 catalogue-workflow pilot: one sale yields £399 gross; a £799 expansion is offered only after input quality and review burden are verified.
- No amount is counted until cash is verifiably received.
