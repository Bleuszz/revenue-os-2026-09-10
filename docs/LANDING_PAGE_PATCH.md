# £149 Landing-Page Conversion Patch

## Target customer

Small UK corporate service, software, or e-commerce businesses that already have a live offer but whose primary landing page makes the buyer work to understand the audience, outcome, proof, or next action.

## Purchase trigger

- An active promotion, launch, paid campaign, seasonal push, or recently updated site.
- A visible high-intent action such as booking, requesting a quote, starting a trial, or buying.
- Public copy that is generic, internally focused, contradictory, or missing a clear next step.

## £149 scope — delivered within 24 hours

- Annotated review of one primary landing page.
- Rewritten hero: audience, outcome, differentiator, and primary call to action.
- Rewritten supporting section, trust/proof structure, and up to five FAQs.
- Implementation-ready copy in Markdown or a structured document.
- One revision pass.
- No website credentials required.

## Optional £249 implementation tier

Offered only when the site technology and edit boundary are verified. Includes the £149 copy package plus a reviewed implementation patch for a simple supported codebase. It excludes design-system replacement, analytics configuration, regulated claims, checkout changes, and production deployment unless separately agreed.

## Value-first preview

Before asking for payment, show three current page-specific observations and one rewritten hero. Label assumptions and request confirmation of customer facts before final delivery.

## Sales process

1. Qualify from a current public page and an observable purchase trigger.
2. Send a concise message with two or three concrete observations.
3. On interest, deliver the one-hero preview and confirm the £149 scope.
4. Collect payment through the user's verified route before completing the remaining package.
5. Deliver within 24 hours, request factual corrections, and include one revision.
6. After acceptance, ask whether a referral or permission to use an anonymised before/after would be appropriate.

## Boundaries

- Do not promise conversion uplift, rankings, traffic, or revenue.
- Do not fabricate testimonials, customer counts, accreditations, urgency, scarcity, or guarantees.
- Do not make medical, legal, financial, environmental, safety, or performance claims without authoritative customer evidence.
- Do not publish or access production systems without explicit scope and authority.
- Treat the client as final approver of every factual claim.

## Initial experiment

- Activation: after the primary 10-contact set has had a 24-hour observation window with no engagement, or earlier if a prospect clearly has a stronger landing-page need.
- First batch: five corporate prospects with live, current, specific evidence.
- Success signal: one meaningful reply or preview request within 48 hours.
- Pause trigger: any complaint or repeated bounce pattern.
- Reassess trigger: five sends with no engagement after 48 hours.
