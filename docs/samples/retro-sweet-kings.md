# Retro Sweet Kings public catalogue preview

Source checked 2026-09-10: https://www.ebay.co.uk/str/msrworld1

## Observed public examples

- A featured title reads `Wedding Favours favour Heart Love Retro Sweets Mallow Valentines Day Mothers Day`, duplicating the favour term.
- Several best-seller titles fill the available display length before completing the last seasonal keyword, ending in fragments such as `Hallow`, `Hall`, or `Fathers D`.
- Many evergreen sweets currently retain Father's Day alongside Halloween and other event terms, reducing space for product-specific terms.

## Safe title treatment

- Remove duplicated singular/plural terms.
- Rotate verified seasonal occasions deliberately rather than stacking every occasion into every title.
- Put brand, product type, flavour, quantity/weight, and current occasion ahead of low-priority generic terms when those facts are verified.

## QA boundary

Do not change allergen, ingredient, weight, brand, expiry, or dietary claims without authoritative product data.
