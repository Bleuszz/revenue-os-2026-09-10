# Totally Floorsome — preliminary landing-page preview

Prepared: 2026-09-10 19:54 Europe/London

This is an unsent preliminary preview based only on the public pages checked during the mission. It is not client-approved, it does not claim a conversion result, and it must be revalidated immediately before any contact.

## Page reviewed

- https://totallyfloorsome.co.uk/
- https://totallyfloorsome.co.uk/contact-us/

## Three current observations

1. Seven benefit bullets appear before the product path, so price matching, samples, design support, installation, sourcing, and commercial expertise all compete for attention.
2. The homepage says the service is for commercial business only while other public copy refers to homes and domestic deliveries, leaving the primary buyer unclear.
3. The contact page reveals useful purchase qualifiers—such as a 50m² minimum for supply-and-fit work—after the visitor has already chosen to enquire.

## Suggested hero

### Commercial flooring supplied and fitted across the UK—around your working week

Compare LVT, carpet tiles, sheet vinyl, rubber, and laminate for offices and commercial sites. Send your plans or request a survey to receive a specification and quote for supply, installation, or both.

**Primary CTA:** Request a commercial flooring survey

**Secondary CTA:** Browse flooring products

**Qualifier near the CTA:** Supply-and-fit projects from 50m². Confirm whether free surveys apply from 100m² before publishing.

## Suggested support sequence

1. Choose the route: supply only, installation, or specification support.
2. Reduce interruption: explain out-of-hours and phased fitting using confirmed operating terms.
3. Make comparison easy: product type, intended use, coverage, lead time, and ex-VAT price.
4. Set expectations: minimum areas, survey threshold, samples, delivery, and quote response time.
5. Add proof only where permissions and project facts are verified.

## Facts requiring client approval

- Whether the business is commercial-only or also actively serves domestic buyers.
- The exact 50m² supply-and-fit minimum and 100m² free-survey threshold.
- Nationwide installation coverage, out-of-hours availability, and delivery thresholds.
- Price-match conditions and any claim concerning large named clients.
