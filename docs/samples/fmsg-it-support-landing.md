# FMSG — preliminary IT-support page preview

Prepared: 2026-09-10 19:54 Europe/London

This is an unsent preliminary preview based only on the public page checked during the mission. It is not client-approved, it does not claim a conversion result, and it must be revalidated immediately before any contact.

## Page reviewed

- https://fmsgltd.com/it-support/

## Three current observations

1. The opening promises dependable expert-led support across all sectors, then places a long enquiry form before the page helps a buyer choose between managed and complementary support.
2. Several later sections repeat related benefits—reduced downtime, improved performance, added capacity, and lower cost—without a short decision framework.
3. The wider navigation spans IT, telecoms, electrical, renewables, fire, security, heating, roofing, and facilities management, which can obscure the next step for an IT-only visitor.

## Suggested hero

### Managed IT support for Midlands organisations that need less downtime and a clear escalation path

Use FMSG as your outsourced helpdesk or extend your internal IT team with co-managed support. Start with a practical review of your users, locations, current support gaps, and required response levels.

**Primary CTA:** Discuss my IT support requirements

**Secondary CTA:** Compare managed and co-managed support

## Suggested support sequence

1. Choose the operating model: fully managed or support for an internal team.
2. Define coverage: users, sites, devices, hours, escalation, and excluded project work.
3. Present outcomes once: resolution, continuity, security, capacity, and cost control.
4. Put numerical SLA and retention evidence beside its definitions and measurement period.
5. Use relevant client proof by sector rather than a long undifferentiated testimonial block.

## Facts requiring client approval

- Exact geographic coverage and whether remote services extend beyond listed counties.
- Helpdesk hours, response definitions, escalation routes, and exclusions.
- The basis and current measurement periods for the 99%, 4.75-second, 97%, and 96% statistics.
- Permission to name every customer or display each accreditation.
