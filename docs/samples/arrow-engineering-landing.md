## Suggested support sequence

1. Start with the buyer's situation: obsolete component, breakdown, modification, prototype, or production batch.
2. Show capability: gears, sprockets, couplings, pulleys, bore and keyway, broaching, and splining—only where the team confirms the current list.
3. Explain what to send: drawing, sample, dimensions, material, tolerance, quantity, and deadline.
4. State the response and emergency-support process using only verified service levels.
5. Place ISO and history proof beside the quote action after confirming current certification wording.

## Facts requiring client approval

- Which work is available 24/7 and what emergency-response commitment applies.
- Materials, tolerances, batch sizes, and services currently handled in-house.
- The `over 40 years` history wording and current ISO 9001 certification.
- Whether customers may safely supply a physical sample and how it is returned.
