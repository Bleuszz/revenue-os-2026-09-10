# Live Innovative Solutions — preliminary homepage preview

Prepared: 2026-09-10 19:54 Europe/London

This is an unsent preliminary preview based only on the public pages checked during the mission. It is not client-approved, it does not claim a conversion result, and it must be revalidated immediately before any contact.

## Page reviewed

- https://liveinnovativesolutions.com/
- https://liveinnovativesolutions.com/contact-us/

## Three current observations

1. The homepage opens with `Welcome to Live Innovative Solutions` and a technology list, but it does not identify the project types, buyer, or delivery outcome in the headline.
2. The first actions are `More About Us` and `Meet the Team`, while the project-enquiry action appears much later.
3. Two long vendor sections separate the services/sectors from the contact action; the existing case-study evidence is not used to help a visitor choose the relevant next step.

## Suggested hero

### Structured cabling, AV, and secure workplace networks—from design to project handover

Plan and deliver the technology infrastructure behind commercial fit-outs, data centres, and multi-site workplaces across the UK and Europe. Start with your site, programme, performance requirements, and current design stage.

**Primary CTA:** Discuss an infrastructure project

**Secondary CTA:** View relevant case studies

## Suggested support sequence

1. Choose the project: office fit-out, data centre, education, government, health, finance, retail, or media.
2. Match services to the project stage: concept, design, installation, testing, handover, and ongoing support.
3. Show one relevant case study with scale, scope, systems, and result that the customer has approved.
4. Condense vendor relationships into proof of capability, supported technologies, and applicable warranties.
5. Ask for site, locations, timeline, drawings, standards, and procurement route in the enquiry.

## Facts requiring client approval

- Geographic coverage and the exact contracting entity for UK versus European work.
- Which design, installation, testing, and support stages are provided for each service.
- Which projects and customer names may be used as public proof.
- When the 25-year vendor-backed warranty applies and which accreditation conditions must be met.
