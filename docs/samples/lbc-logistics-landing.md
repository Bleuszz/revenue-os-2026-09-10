# LBC Logistics — preliminary landing-page preview

Prepared: 2026-09-10 19:54 Europe/London

This is an unsent preliminary preview based only on the public pages checked during the mission. It is not client-approved, it does not claim a conversion result, and it must be revalidated immediately before any contact.

## Page reviewed

- https://www.lbclogistics.co.uk/
- https://www.lbclogistics.co.uk/contact

## Three current observations

1. The opening stacks `A greener way to deliver`, `Milton Keynes' Only Zero-Emissions Courier Fleet`, `Same-Day Courier Service Available`, and a separate `Welcome` heading. The strongest purchase facts compete instead of forming one clear promise.
2. The same-day action is visible, but `Quote & Book` does not tell the reader what information is needed or what happens next.
3. The contact page repeats `Send Us a Message` and contains visible confirmation-copy errors, which can reduce confidence at the point of enquiry.

## Suggested hero

### Same-day UK courier deliveries from Milton Keynes—handled by an all-electric commercial fleet

Send secure documents, parcels, or pallets with a courier team that offers same-day collection and nationwide delivery. Tell us what is moving, where it needs to go, and when it must arrive to get a tailored quote.

**Primary CTA:** Quote my same-day delivery

**Secondary CTA:** See our zero-emissions courier service

## Suggested support sequence

1. What can be carried: documents, parcels, pallets, and any exclusions the company confirms.
2. Where and when: Milton Keynes base, UK-wide operation, same-day availability subject to confirmation.
3. Why LBC: all-electric commercial fleet and verified experience claim after client approval.
4. How quoting works: collection postcode, delivery postcode, dimensions/weight, deadline, and contact details.
5. Proof: use only permissioned case-study facts and customer names.

## Facts requiring client approval

- Whether `same-day` applies nationally or only to specified collection areas and cutoff times.
- Whether `Milton Keynes' only` remains current and is supportable.
- Which cargo types, maximum weights, insurance limits, and service exclusions apply.
- The public claim of more than 16 years of courier experience.
