# Landing-page patch — illustrative example

This is a reusable demonstration of the deliverable format. It is not a real client engagement, testimonial, performance result, or claim that any conversion improvement occurred.

## Example context

Assumed business: a UK bookkeeping firm serving independent e-commerce businesses.

Assumed action: book a 20-minute fit call.

These assumptions would have to be replaced with verified prospect facts in a real preview.

## Before

> Quality financial solutions for businesses of all sizes
>
> We offer a complete range of professional services tailored to your needs.
>
> Learn more

## Diagnosis

1. `Businesses of all sizes` does not tell the reader whether the service is for them.
2. `Quality financial solutions` describes neither the problem nor the concrete outcome.
3. `Complete range` increases perceived scope instead of making the next purchase easier.
4. `Learn more` does not explain what happens after the click or why the visitor should act now.
5. No proof mechanism or risk-reducing detail is visible near the decision.

## After — example hero

### Bookkeeping for UK e-commerce teams that need clean monthly numbers—not another spreadsheet chase

Get reconciled accounts, a simple cash view, and a clear month-end checklist from a named bookkeeping contact. Start with a 20-minute fit call to confirm your sales channels, current backlog, and the right scope.

**Primary CTA:** Book a 20-minute fit call

**Secondary CTA:** See exactly what the monthly service includes

## Supporting structure

### Know what is reconciled, what needs attention, and what happens next

- Sales-channel and bank reconciliation based on the agreed scope.
- A concise monthly exceptions list instead of unexplained adjustments.
- A repeatable document deadline and month-end handoff.

### Suggested proof block

Use only verified evidence supplied by the business, such as named qualifications, supported software certifications, a permissioned customer quote, or a factual service-level commitment. If none exists, omit the block rather than inventing proof.

### Suggested FAQ set

1. Which sales channels and bookkeeping platforms do you support?
2. What do you need from us each month?
3. Can you take over from another bookkeeper or clear a backlog?
4. What is included, and what is billed separately?
5. What happens during the fit call?

## Handoff format for a real customer

Each section is recorded with the exact current text, a ready-to-paste recommendation, the reason for the change, and the facts the customer must approve.

- Hero: audience, services, outcome, and timing.
- CTA: the actual booking or purchase flow and what happens next.
- Proof: permission and factual accuracy for every proof element.
- FAQ: policies, scope boundaries, and the answers to verified purchase objections.
