# Secondary-offer qualification rubric

Use this only when the landing-page patch activation condition in `LANDING_PAGE_PATCH.md` is met.

## Mandatory gates

- UK limited company or similarly clear corporate subscriber.
- Current public corporate contact channel.
- Live page checked during the mission.
- Specific high-intent action visible: buy, book, quote, trial, demo, or enquiry.
- At least two concrete copy/structure issues that can be explained without guessing analytics.
- No personal, medical, legal, investment, or otherwise regulated claim work.
- No suppression record or previous adverse signal.

## Score — 100 points

- Current purchase trigger: 20
- Clarity and urgency of the observed problem: 20
- Ability to show a credible preview before payment: 20
- Likely value of one conversion relative to £149 price: 15
- Public corporate channel quality: 10
- Trust fit with available proof: 10
- Fulfilment within 24 hours: 5

Only contact prospects scoring at least 75. Record the page, evidence, score, assumptions, and contact route in `ops/LEADS.csv` before sending.

## Disqualifiers

- The apparent problem depends on private analytics that are unavailable.
- The page is a dormant placeholder or appears abandoned.
- The mailbox is evidently personal and corporate-subscriber status is unclear.
- The business would require unsupported regulated claims or deceptive urgency.
- The change would require account access before the prospect has expressed interest.
